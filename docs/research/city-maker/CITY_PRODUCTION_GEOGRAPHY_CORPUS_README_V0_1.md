# CityMaker production geography corpus v0.1

24 synthetic known-answer cases exercise the spatial decision adapter contract for owner evidence across land/rights, productive capability, suppliers, workforce access, freight delivery, utilities, material qualification and community/environment constraints.

The evaluator must not recompute the owner's result. It checks exact owner, contract, source revision, scope, candidate subject and current evidence. A current hard-gate failure makes the candidate Infeasible. Missing, stale, revoked, unknown or mismatched evidence yields Unknown. Scope mismatch yields NotComparable. Schema/identity violations yield Unsupported.

This is not a site-selection optimizer, route engine, production network, capability closure engine, resource ledger, planning authority or community-consent proof. All values are synthetic.
