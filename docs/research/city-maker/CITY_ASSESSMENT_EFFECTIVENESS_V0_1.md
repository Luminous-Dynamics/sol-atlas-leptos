# CityMaker assessment effectiveness profile v0.1

## Purpose

Assess the **assessment and decision system**, not the sustainability of the district directly. District outcomes and assessment-system effectiveness are separate dimensions. A high score in one cannot compensate for failure in the other.

## Research anchor

Riaz, Matthews and Dedekorkut-Howes (first published 27 August 2026), *Beyond the Checklist: Meta-Indicators for Evaluating Effectiveness of Neighbourhood Sustainability Assessment Tools*, Sustainable Development, DOI 10.1002/sd.71602: https://doi.org/10.1002/sd.71602.

The paper develops 13 meta-indicators across performance, applicability and operational efficiency through a document-based comparison of eight neighbourhood assessment tools, interpreted against ISO 37101, ISO 37105, ISO 37123 and ISO 37125. The authors state the framework is conceptual and has not yet been empirically validated. CityMaker treats it as a research reference, not a certification or ready-made rating.

## The 13 meta-indicators

| Dimension | ID | Meta-indicator | CityMaker question |
|---|---|---|---|
| Performance | ME-01 | Sustainability Dimension Coverage | Are environmental, social, economic, institutional and technological issues represented without important omissions? |
| Performance | ME-02 | Resilience and Risk Integration | Are risk, continuity, preparedness and recovery integrated into core decision criteria? |
| Performance | ME-03 | Indicator Transparency and Clarity | Are definitions, units, boundaries, thresholds, calculation and evidence requirements reproducible? |
| Performance | ME-04 | Equity and Inclusion | Are access, affordability, disability, vulnerable groups, displacement and distributional burdens visible? |
| Performance | ME-05 | Innovation and Technology Inclusion | Are digital and technical methods linked to measurable outcomes, governance, interoperability and maintenance? |
| Applicability | ME-06 | Governance and Policy Integration | Does the method align with local planning, legal processes, decision rights and budgets? |
| Applicability | ME-07 | Stakeholder Participation | Can affected people and workers influence requirements, alternatives and corrective actions? |
| Applicability | ME-08 | Adaptability and Contextual Fit | Can the method respond to local conditions and data limitations without breaking comparability? |
| Applicability | ME-09 | Cross-Sectoral Relevance | Are essential services, infrastructure, production and lifecycle dependencies evaluated together? |
| Operational efficiency | ME-10 | Scalability and Usability | Is the method reproducible and proportionate at component, building, district and city scales? |
| Operational efficiency | ME-11 | Training and Implementation Support | Are role-based skills, training, support and local maintenance needs explicit? |
| Operational efficiency | ME-12 | Cost and Time Efficiency | Are all assessment costs and time burdens visible, including data, consultation, review and monitoring? |
| Operational efficiency | ME-13 | Monitoring and Post-Evaluation | Are outcomes measured after handover, linked back to decisions and corrected when they diverge? |

## Evidence maturity

Each meta-indicator uses an ordinal evidence state:

- `NotAddressed`: no evidence the system addresses the issue.
- `Mentioned`: theme appears, but no definition or operational method.
- `Defined`: a clear rule and evidence requirement exist.
- `Operationalized`: a repeatable procedure is used in a defined workflow.
- `Measured`: actual implementation measurements and denominators exist.
- `IndependentlyValidated`: independent reviewers or replication support the result.

These are evidence maturity categories, not numeric scores. Do not sum them or assign arbitrary weights to produce a CityMaker rating. Missing evidence remains Unknown. “Not found in manual” means not evidenced, not proof of absence.

## Comparative sample

The reference paper compares LEED-ND, BREEAM Communities, Green Star Communities, DGNB Districts, CASBEE-UD, Green Mark for Districts, GBI Township and HK-BEAM Plus Neighbourhood. CityMaker should compare exact manual editions, regions, eligibility, evidence burdens, assessor roles, costs and update cycles. It should not rank these tools from the names or copy their weights.

The paper reports that broad district schemes tend to include governance, liveability, mobility and public realm alongside environmental criteria; more technically focused schemes may need supplementary treatment of stakeholder participation, governance and monitoring. These are hypotheses to test against current official manuals, not an immutable ordering.

## Standards relationship

ISO 37101, ISO 37105, ISO 37123 and ISO 37125 are reference lenses for governance, urban-system description/interoperability, resilience and ESG-style reporting. The 13 meta-indicators are derived by the paper's interpretation of ISO guidance; they are not prescribed directly by those standards. This profile is not a South African legal compliance checklist.

## Reproducible evaluation protocol

1. Freeze each assessment tool's exact manual edition, region, retrieval date and source licence.
2. Extract indicator definitions, mandatory/optional status, units, scoring, data requirements, assessor roles, training, cost, time and monitoring obligations.
3. Have two reviewers independently code at least two manuals; publish raw agreement, disagreement examples and adjudication.
4. Invite affected residents, workers, operators, local institutions and relevant professionals to review indicator relevance and implementation burden.
5. Identify local additions for Johannesburg/Gauteng and South Africa, but retain the original comparator definitions.
6. Apply the profile first to the synthetic CityMaker reference district, and later to a rights-cleared real pilot with public and community review.
7. Publish evidence gaps, costs, time, training and monitoring readiness separately. Do not emit one composite score.

## Claim boundary

This profile evaluates CityMaker as an assessment system. It does not establish the quality of a district, certify compliance, establish community consent or prove any urban form is optimal. Empirical validation, inter-rater testing and stakeholder review remain required.
