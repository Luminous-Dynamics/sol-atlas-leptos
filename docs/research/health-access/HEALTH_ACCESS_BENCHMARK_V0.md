# Healthcare Access Q0 Reference Audit — v0.1

**Status:** synthetic software-contract research only. No live facility data, patient data, clinical decision, or operational navigation is included.

## Goal

Evaluate verified, reachable, service-specific capacity rather than treating a facility's coordinate or listing as proof of usable healthcare access.

## Paradigm shift

Healthcare access is a time-bounded, evidence-bearing service-chain property:
- the requested service must match;
- availability must be explicitly known available;
- readiness must be explicitly known ready;
- evidence must be sourced and within a declared freshness window;
- the facility must be open at the evaluation time;
- a route composed of available edges must exist within the travel-time budget;
- only then may the service's declared capacity contribute to the result.

Unknown evidence is neither success nor failure; it cannot count as confirmed capacity. Disconnected demand remains unserved even if a disconnected region has surplus capacity.

The Rust reference evaluator is intentionally small and isolated in its own Cargo workspace. It is not integrated into the production Sol-Atlas planner. Frozen expected outcomes are authored in the fixture and compared with implementation output; they are not generated by the evaluator at test time.

## Proposed Q0 acceptance targets (unmeasured)

- 100% exact agreement with every frozen expected result.
- 100% of unavailable, unknown, stale, out-of-window, unsupported-service, not-ready, disconnected, or over-budget facilities excluded from confirmed capacity.
- Zero phantom capacity in negative controls.
- Deterministic results on pinned fixture bytes.
- Mutation tests confirm that degrading evidence or blocking all paths cannot increase eligible capacity.
- A receipt includes code revision, fixture digest, case count, result, and an explicit synthetic-only claim ceiling.

A Q0 PASS means only that the specified synthetic software contract passed on the exact tested source revision. It does not establish facility facts, clinical quality, real-world travel times, demand estimation, fairness, or health outcomes.

## Industry and research comparators

WHO SARA and HHFA separate facility/service availability and readiness from broader quality-of-care and management indicators. They should inform the source model but do not themselves establish route accessibility. Compare nearest-facility distance as a baseline, network travel time as the next baseline, and this contract's capacity/readiness/freshness-aware result as an additional layer.
