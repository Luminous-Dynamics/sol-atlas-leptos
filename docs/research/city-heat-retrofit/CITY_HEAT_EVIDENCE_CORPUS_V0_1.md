# City heat evidence corpus v0.1

26 synthetic cases exercise claim admission for the city heat/retrofit atlas. The oracle must not calculate climate exposure or thermal physics. It checks whether the evidence supplied is sufficient for a requested claim level H0–H5.

Key negative controls:
- TMY cannot be offered as a heatwave;
- future and observed-event weather require their explicit periods/scenario;
- ward-level population cannot be promoted to a building count;
- survey samples cannot be presented as census;
- model scope, convergence and required thermal-service outputs are mandatory;
- modelled values cannot be relabelled as measured;
- sensor calibration, data quality, consent and aggregation are required;
- predicted or merely installed interventions cannot support accepted causal effect claims;
- missing source rights block redistribution;
- natural ventilation is not treated as available when the scenario says it is unusable.

Incomplete cohort coverage can be admissible only when the unknown fraction is reported; it is never silently treated as zero.

This is not real Johannesburg exposure or retrofit evidence.
